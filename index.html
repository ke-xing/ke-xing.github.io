First-year PhD Student in Computer Science

Beijing Jiaotong University · Beijing, China

I am a first-year PhD student at the School of Computer Science & Technology, Beijing Jiaotong University, advised by Prof. Xiaojie Jin.

My current research focuses on video world models: learning to generate and model dynamic visual worlds. I am particularly interested in memory and persistent world states for long-horizon interactions.

Previously, I worked on geometry-aware stereoscopic video generation, panoramic image and video generation, and immersive 4D scene reconstruction. These experiences shape my interest in connecting video generation with spatial understanding and temporally consistent world modeling.
